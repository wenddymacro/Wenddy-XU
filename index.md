<section class="card" data-lang="zh">
  <h2>代表成果（节选）</h2>
  <p>以下论文聚焦中国宏观经济、因果推断与政策评估，涵盖DSGE建模、DID及相关经验研究方法。</p>
  <ul class="item-list">
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S009506962400189X">Patronage and pollution (JEEM, 2025)</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：研究“关系网络”在不同晋升激励下对污染排放的影响，发现将环保纳入考核后，关系网络由促污转为减污，但伴随一定增长权衡。</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S016726812400492X?dgcid=coauthor">Television exposure in early childhood and subsequent cognitive outcomes (JEBO, 2025)</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：基于中国农村数据识别早期电视接触对后续认知的影响，结果显示0-5岁阶段的完整电视暴露显著提升10-15岁认知得分。</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2444569X25001143">Impact of ICT development on China's carbon emissions (Journal of Innovation & Knowledge, 2025)</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：ICT发展在空间上表现为“本地增排、周边减排、总体减排”，并可通过技术创新与产业升级间接促进减排。</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4788180">Baby Boom (SSRN, 2024)</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：利用县志手工数据和三重差分方法评估家庭联产承包责任制对生育率的影响，发现改革显著提高生育，尤其体现在二孩及以上。</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s11149-022-09455-6">Environmental regulation and energy efficiency (Journal of Regulatory Economics)</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：以“按日连续处罚”政策为准自然实验，发现该政策显著提升企业能源效率，并同时带来环境与产出双重改善。</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eap.2023.03.005">Do VAT cuts help stabilize employment? (Economic Analysis and Policy)</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：围绕增值税税率改革进行DID分析，发现减税并不必然稳就业；非劳动要素相对价格下降会强化“资本替代劳动”机制。</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/93167/1/MPRA_paper_93167.pdf">A Baseline Medium-Scale NK DSGE Model for Policy Analysis</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：构建中等规模新凯恩斯DSGE基准框架，用于联合分析货币政策与财政政策冲击及其传导机制。</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/109234/">A Baseline DSGE Model of Climate Change for Climate Policy Analysis</a>
      <p class="item-note">摘要：在NK-DSGE中引入碳循环与气候模块，比对税、总量控制等多类气候政策对宏观经济与环境变量的动态影响。</p>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="card" data-lang="en">
  <h2>Selected Publications</h2>
  <p>These papers focus on China's macroeconomy, causal inference, and policy evaluation, including DSGE modeling, DID, and applied empirical methods.</p>
  <ul class="item-list">
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S009506962400189X">Patronage and pollution (JEEM, 2025)</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: This paper studies how patronage affects emissions under different promotion incentives, showing that environmental KPIs can turn patronage from pollution-increasing to pollution-reducing.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S016726812400492X?dgcid=coauthor">Television exposure in early childhood and subsequent cognitive outcomes (JEBO, 2025)</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: Using rural China data, the study finds that full TV exposure during ages 0-5 significantly improves cognitive outcomes at ages 10-15.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2444569X25001143">Impact of ICT development on China's carbon emissions (Journal of Innovation & Knowledge, 2025)</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: ICT development has heterogeneous spatial effects and can reduce aggregate emissions through technological innovation and industrial upgrading channels.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4788180">Baby Boom (SSRN, 2024)</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: With manually collected county-level records and a triple-difference design, the paper shows that the HRS reform raised fertility, especially for higher-order births.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s11149-022-09455-6">Environmental regulation and energy efficiency (Journal of Regulatory Economics)</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: The Daily Penalty Policy is shown to improve firm energy efficiency and deliver both environmental and economic gains.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eap.2023.03.005">Do VAT cuts help stabilize employment? (Economic Analysis and Policy)</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: DID evidence suggests VAT rate cuts may reduce employment when cheaper non-labor inputs increase substitution away from labor.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/93167/1/MPRA_paper_93167.pdf">A Baseline Medium-Scale NK DSGE Model for Policy Analysis</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: A medium-scale NK DSGE baseline is developed for joint monetary-fiscal policy analysis and impulse-response-based interpretation.</p>
    </li>
    <li>
      <a href="https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/109234/">A Baseline DSGE Model of Climate Change for Climate Policy Analysis</a>
      <p class="item-note">Abstract: The model extends NK DSGE with geophysical blocks to compare macro and environmental dynamics under multiple climate policy regimes.</p>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>

<script>
function setLang(lang) {
  document.querySelectorAll('[data-lang]').forEach(function (el) {
    el.classList.toggle('show', el.getAttribute('data-lang') === lang);
  });
  document.getElementById('btn-zh').classList.toggle('active', lang === 'zh');
  document.getElementById('btn-en').classList.toggle('active', lang === 'en');
  try { localStorage.setItem('homepage-lang', lang); } catch (e) {}
}

(function initLang() {
  var lang = 'zh';
  try {
    lang = localStorage.getItem('homepage-lang') || ((navigator.language || '').toLowerCase().indexOf('zh') === 0 ? 'zh' : 'en');
  } catch (e) {}
  setLang(lang === 'en' ? 'en' : 'zh');
})();
</script>
